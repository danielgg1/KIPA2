NEW KNOWLEDGE RULES

1 If the case is about agreement of dismissal, and the employee has not come to work at least 3 months without reason,
    then conclusion: Juridical advisor should advise to fire employee without any compensation of transmission.

2 If the case is about agreement of dismissal, but the employee has an occupational disability:
    then conclusion: Juridical advisor should advise to give the employee the maximum compensation of transmission.

3 If the case is about agreement of dismissal, but there is no juridical ground for firing the employee:
    then conclusion: Juridical advisor should advise to give the employee the maximum compensation of transmission.

4 If the case is about agreement of dismissal, and there is sufficient juridical ground for firing the employee:
    then conclusion: Juridical advisor should advise to give the employee no compensation of transmission.

5 If the case is a complaint, and the employee has had earlier problematic behavior, 
    then conclusion: Juridical advisor should advise to go to court.

6 If a complaint is about violence on the workplace, 
    then conclusion: Juridical advisor should advise to go to court.

7 If the case is a complaint, but it is not about violence on the workplace, 
    then conclusion: Juridical advisor should start mediation process.

8 If the warning is severe,
    then conclusion: Juridical advisor should advise to fire the employee.

9 If the case is the third warning in a row, 
    then conclusion: Juridical advisor should advise salary suspension.

10 If the case is a warning (but it is not severe and not third time in a row), 
    then conclusion: Juridical advisor should advise to send a warning letter.

11 If the case is an improvement procedure, 
    then conclusion: Juridical advisor should advise to start mediation process.
